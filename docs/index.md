# Lectio SA

Offline South African Catholic daily readings for Android.

Lectio SA keeps the bundled Scripture text and reviewed calendar data on your device, so the daily reading experience does not require an internet
connection, an account, or advertising.

## What it includes

- South African Catholic calendar coverage for 2026 and 2027
- Daily readings, psalms, alternatives, and Mass-form selections where
  reviewed data is available
- Offline Scripture lookup using the World English Bible Catholic edition
- Date navigation, calendar browsing, and remembered reading position
- Light, dark, and system themes
- Adjustable text size and optional verse numbers
- Sources, coverage, rights, content-version, and build information in the app

## Privacy

Lectio SA is designed to work locally on your device and does not use
advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or an application server.

Read the [Lectio SA Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
